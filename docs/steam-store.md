## About this game

**The dungeon goes down forever. You go down with it.**

Click to fight, and hire companions with the gold that falls. Thirty-two of them, from a squire with a rusty sword to
things that came down for the dark and stayed for the company. Level them into the thousands. Pick a hero and lead
them around the room yourself, or sit back and let the party work.

**Ascend.** When the floors stop giving, climb back up. Souls summon Spirits: the Sleeper hits harder while you're
away, the Wither shrinks bosses, the Lure brings treasure goblins faster. Every run back down is quicker than the last.

**Awaken the Heart.** Past floor 350, something under the Keep starts beating. Give up everything for bound souls, and
the eight bosses of the dungeon come back bound to you.

**Every monster has a card.** One in every color the zones come back in, and a gold copy from
champions. Most drops are one in a million, and every zone you finish in a color makes you stronger. Send a strong
companion on an expedition to hunt the floor you want, in real time, and see what they bring home.

**And the rest**

- Treasure goblins running for the exit with your gold. Some of them are rainbows.
- Bosses on a timer, champions with gold cards, and a wall past floor 400.
- The Keep: walk your hero around the castle above the dungeon, between the shrines, the Heart and the ladder down.
- 75 achievements, 30 cursors to unlock, and numbers that keep climbing past 1e308.
- Plays on while you're away.
