Rampage Rework

Rampage, reworked

Ten of the companions with the game's own sprites, playing a Rampage on loop: its start, a tier-up halfway through, and its end. The top row is today's effect, the bottom row the new one.

Now · one ring each
New · burst, glow, embers, eyes

  • Floor burst

    A ring blasts out along the floor under each companion, rolling outward from the middle of the party.

  • Red outline

    Every companion gets a pulsing red rim for as long as the Rampage lasts.

  • Embers

    Sparks rise off the party the whole time, twice as many after a tier-up.

  • Red eyes

    Eyes, visor slits and carved pumpkin eyes burn red and glow, then cool back down when it ends.
