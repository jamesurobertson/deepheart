# Launch price options

For the Steam store page and press kit. Figures are general indie Steam guidance (2026 pricing guides, Steam data 2023–25).
No source covered idle or incremental games specifically, so check a few comparable titles on Steam before locking in.

## Genre and market numbers
- Median launch price of the top 50 new Steam releases by revenue: about $15.64, down from about $19.50.
- Polished indie single-player: $14.99–$19.99. Viral or multiplayer-first: $7.99–$9.99.
- $5–$8 is not a safe default; $4.99 sits well below the usual range.
- Common launch discount: 10–20%, often 10–15% for the first two weeks, or none.

## James's plan: $4.99 with a December launch discount
| Step | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Base | $4.99 |
| 10% launch discount | $4.49 |
| 20% launch discount | $3.99 |
| 50% sale later | $2.49 |

- At $4.99 a deep sale later leaves little room; a 10–20% launch discount is the safe depth.
- December overlaps the Steam Winter Sale. Check Steamworks for sale dates and discount-timing rules before picking an end date.

## Alternatives
- $7.99 base, 15% launch discount ($6.79): more room for later sales, further from the idle-game norm.

## Decision for James
Base price, launch discount depth, and its end date.
