## One line
Fight monsters, hire companions with the gold they earn, and descend the dungeon for souls.

## Short description
Deepheart is an idle dungeon crawler in 3D. Your party fights for you, you spend the gold on more companions, and every
run back down is faster than the last. Collect a card for every monster, send hunters on expeditions, and keep
descending: there is no bottom.

## Features
- A party that fights for you. Hire 32 companions, level them into the thousands, and lead a hero around the Keep with
  your mouse or a finger.
- Every monster has a card, and a gold copy of each. Complete a set for +25% damage, or +50% for gold.
- Expeditions. Send a strong companion to hunt a floor's cards while you play, or while you're away.
- Two prestige layers: ascend for souls, awaken the Heart for heartstones.
- Treasure goblins that run for the exit with your loot. Every so often one is a rainbow.
- No bottom. Eight zones come back corrupted, abyssal, hollow and eternal, and the numbers climb past 1e308.
