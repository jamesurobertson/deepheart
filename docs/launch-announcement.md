## Body
Deepheart is live.

Your party fights for you. You spend the gold on more companions, ascend for souls, and every run back down is faster
than the last. There is no bottom.

What's in it:
- 32 companions to hire and level into the thousands
- A card for every monster, with gold copies from champions
- Expeditions that hunt cards while you play, or while you're away
- Two prestige layers: ascend for souls, awaken the Heart for bound souls
- Treasure goblins that run for the exit with your loot
